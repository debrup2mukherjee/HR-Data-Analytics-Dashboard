# HR Analytics Dashboard - Comprehensive Workforce Intelligence

## Project Overview

This dashboard was developed to analyze and monitor comprehensive workforce metrics and HR performance. It provides a complete overview of employee distribution, compensation analysis, gender diversity, departmental performance, and salary trends using interactive Excel visualizations.

The dashboard helps businesses:

- Track overall workforce composition and headcount
- Monitor compensation trends and salary distribution
- Evaluate gender diversity across departments
- Analyze employee distribution across locations
- Identify workforce utilization patterns (FTE vs Contract vs Intern)
- Make data-driven HR decisions and strategic workforce planning

## Tools & Technologies Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Power Pivot
- DAX Formulas
- Conditional Formatting
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Interactive Dashboard Designing

### Average Salary Trends

The area chart displays salary progression over time (2018-2026).

**Key Observations:**
- Steady salary growth from 2018 to 2019
- Slight dip in 2020 (possibly pandemic impact)
- Strong recovery and growth from 2023 onwards
- Peak salary levels projected for 2026 at Rs 1,20,000+

This helps track compensation inflation and budget planning.

### Salary Range Distribution (Below 45k vs 45k-65k vs Above 65k)

## Final Business Insights

### Major Findings

#### Workforce Composition Insights
- Total headcount of 222 employees with balanced distribution across departments
- 64% FTE rate indicates strong core workforce with flexible talent pool
- Geographic concentration in Delhi suggests centralized operations

#### Compensation Insights
- Average salary of Rs 75,011 is competitive
- Salary range from Rs 29,775 to Rs 1,24,698 shows wide compensation bands
- Engineering department commands highest total compensation
- Steady salary growth trend from 2018-2026 indicates consistent investments in talent

#### Diversity & Inclusion Insights
- 45% female representation demonstrates strong diversity efforts
- Gender distribution varies significantly across departments
- Some departments (Engineering) need focused diversity improvement

#### Departmental Performance Insights
- Human Resources has the largest team (26 employees)
- Engineering receives highest compensation investment (Rs 1,917k)
- Average experience of 4 years suggests a mid-level experienced workforce

## Business Recommendations

1. **Improve Gender Diversity**: Focus on increasing female representation in Engineering and technical departments through targeted recruitment

2. **Salary Equity Review**: Analyze salary gaps within departments to ensure fair compensation for similar roles

3. **Talent Retention**: With 44% contract employees, develop conversion programs to retain high performers

4. **Geographic Expansion**: Consider distributing workforce across more locations to tap into regional talent pools

5. **Experience Building**: Average experience of 4 years suggests need for senior talent acquisition in key departments

6. **Compensation Benchmarking**: Continue monitoring salary trends to remain competitive in the market

7. **Department Balancing**: Review headcount distribution to ensure optimal team sizes aligned with business priorities

8. **FTE Optimization**: Evaluate the 64% FTE ratio against business needs and industry standards
